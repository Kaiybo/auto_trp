# Changelog

## 0.24.0 (2026-11-27): True Colours

- **Fixed: Currently and the glances left empty while out of character.** With "Pause Currently and glances while out of character" on, a profile set out of character in Total RP 3 never had *Currently* or the Mood, Lately, Company and Marks glances written at all, so they stayed blank (only Ambition, written with the story, got through). A pause now holds what's there but still fills anything empty, so all five At first glance slots and *Currently* always have something in them. Text you've cleared or written yourself is still left alone.
- **The profile icon is who you are.** It used to follow your nickname (a coin for *Mine-Warden*, a mask for *VanCleef's Bane*). Now it's the talent tree you've put the most into, with a clear lead: Starfall for a Balance druid, the bear for a Feral one, Healing Touch for Restoration, Shadow Word: Pain for a Shadow priest, the inner rage of Fury, and so on for every class and tree. Before any talents (or with two trees level), it's the face of your people, Total RP 3's own race and body portrait, where the game has it; else your class crest. An icon this client hasn't got is never written: the game's own icon for the tree takes its place.

## 0.23.0 (2026-11-27): Second Wind

### Your calling

- **The work only your class does, recorded.** A new record (`Sources/Calling.lua`) keeps, from your own spells and the game's events alone: a warrior's time in each stance, charges and intercepts, Shield Walls and Last Stands on low health; a rogue's pockets picked (with the coin and things lifted), locks picked, poisons put on a blade (not those mixed at the trade) and Vanishes in time; a hunter's beasts tamed by kind, tracking, traps and Feign Deaths that saved them; a mage's portals and how many went through, teleports, conjured food and water handed over in trades, Polymorphs and Blinks out of trouble; a warlock's summonings, soulstones on others, demons by kind, healthstones made and handed over; a priest's resurrections (strangers apart), shields and Fortitude on others, Mind Control; a shaman's totems by element, Ghost Wolf time, Reincarnations (from the ankh gone) and Ancestral Spirits; a paladin's blessings on others, Redemption, Lay on Hands and Divine Intervention; a druid's time in each shape, Rebirths, Innervates and Thorns on others; a death knight's Death Grips, ghouls and presences. Spells are sorted once by ID; the combat log bails out unless it's your spell on another player; nobody else is kept. Bounded: 12 beasts, 12 kinds of each thing, the 40 latest notable acts.
- **Written only as far as the counts go.** Personality gets one or two habits of the calling (*"Has resurrected more strangers than most priests would bother to."*, *"Fights from a defensive stance by habit, as if someone always needs covering."*, *"Thinks nothing of opening a portal for a group, and has done it twelve times."*), never two opening the same way. History's news tells a beast tamed, portals opened, a summoning, the fallen raised, a sacrifice, a Reincarnation, a narrow escape or a new demon (*"Aldric raised eight strangers where they fell"*), and the longer record the counts (*"he's picked thirty locks, lifted what he could from sixty pockets"*). Another class's acts are never told as yours.
- **Glances.** Lately: *A New Companion*, *Opened the Way*, *A Summoning*, *Raised the Fallen*, *A Soulstone Given*, *A Sacrifice*, *Laid on Hands*, *Back From Death*, *A Narrow Escape*, *Bread and Water*. Marks: *Poison-Stained*, *Light Fingers*, *Freshly Conjured*, *Brimstone*, *Drawn and Tired*, *Hard Pressed*. Four to six ways of saying each.
- **Four new statistics**, each with its own switch and tooltip: *Calling* (*"5 beasts tamed, the latest a cat; 60 traps laid"*), *Narrow escapes* (*"12 behind Shield Wall"*), *Given to others* (*"8 raised, all of them strangers; 120 shields"*) and *Stances and forms* (*"Cat Form 12 hours, Bear Form 2 hours"*). A class with nothing of the kind has the entry left off.
### The shape of their time

- **A record of your time** (new: `Sources/Seasons.lua`, `AutoTRPCharDB.seasons`). Auto TRP keeps each session (start, end, length, from login and logout and a once-a-minute ticker), the game-clock hours you're usually abroad, the real days of the week you come, your longest stretch and longest absence, the return after one, runs of days played, and the first day on record (when Auto TRP first saw you, or the earliest any source has). Bounded: 30 sessions, 14 months, 24 festivals.
- **History** tells it, from facts: *"Is most often abroad after dark."* (or an early riser, a daytime traveller, an evening one), *"Is mostly seen at the end of the week."*, *"It's a year this week since Aldric first set out."* (a month, a hundred days, half a year, each year), *"A month ago this week, Aldric rose to Templar."*, *"Came back after eleven days away."*, *"Spent Hallow's End in Undercity."*, *"Has been on the road nine days running."*, the longest stretch and a long absence once. Four to six ways to say each.
- **Festivals actually kept**: a holiday buff or debuff, quest, item or place seen while the festival was on (Winter Veil, the Lunar Festival, Love is in the Air, Noblegarden, Children's Week, Midsummer, the Harvest Festival, Brewfest, Hallow's End, the Day of the Dead), with where you spent most of it; Darkmoon Faire visits by its grounds or Sayge's fortunes, once a day.
- **How the months went**: the mood journal's days are folded in by month, so History can say the season or month just past was a hard or a good one (*"Had a hard autumn of it."*).
- **Lately** shows an anniversary this week (*A Year On*, *A Month On*), a festival you're keeping today, a day at the Darkmoon Faire and a return after days away. The **mood** lifts a little (proud on an anniversary, merry at a festival or the faire, glad to be home after two weeks or more away), and the **journal** gets a line for each (*"It's a year this week since I set out."*).
- **Two statistics**: *On the road since* (*"The 3rd of March, 2026"*) and *Festivals kept* (*"2: Winter Veil and Hallow's End; the Darkmoon Faire once"*), each with its own switch and tooltip.
- A new setting, "Anniversaries, festivals kept and returns from time away, in History and Lately", on by default.

### Your people and your homeland

- **A record of your people.** A new record (`Sources/Kin.lua`), from game events alone: time played in your race's capital and home lands against time abroad, homecomings (back in the capital after three days or more away, which also lifts the mood), racial gifts used and when one mattered (Will of the Forsaken out of a fear, charm or sleep; Escape Artist out of a root or snare; Stoneform against a poison or bleed; Shadowmeld mid-fight; Blood Fury, Berserking and War Stomp with the fight going badly; Cannibalize to mend), the language you speak aloud in (counted from the chat event, the words never read), your home city's standing (read outside a fight), the places that matter to your people, and kills of your people's old enemies by the creature's name (the Scarlet Crusade, the Scourge and worgen for the Forsaken; satyrs, Warsong orcs and corrupted furbolgs for night elves; Dark Irons, troggs and Frostmane for dwarves; centaurs, the Grimtotem, the Venture Company and quilboar for tauren; and more for every race). Every list is capped; the clock is a thirty-second tick, and the combat log handler leaves at once on anything but three sub-events.
- **Written from it**: History news (*"came home to Ironforge after twelve days away"*, *"made the journey to the Ruins of Lordaeron"*, *"threw off a fear with the will of the Forsaken"*) and the longer record (*"gone home to Thunder Bluff three times after long roads"*, *"cut down more than a hundred of the Scarlet Crusade"*); Lately (*Home Again*, four or five ways for each race; a place of your people told by its feeling, *Old Sorrows*, *Old Enemies*, *Kindred Ground*, *Holy Ground* and more; a gift that mattered); Marks (*Fed on the Fallen*, *Stone-Skinned*, *Blood Fury*, *Berserk*, *Dust and Thunder*); Personality once the record backs it (*"Hasn't set foot in Ironforge in over a month."*, *"Rarely strays far from Mulgore."*, *"Lives on the road, a long way from Teldrassil and the forests of home."*, *"Has earned the trust of Darnassus."*, *"Has a long quarrel with the Scarlet Crusade, and keeps it up."*, a gift used often); Physical's voice by the tongue you speak (*"Says most of what he says aloud in Dwarvish."*, *"Sticks to Common, though he knows Darnassian."*) and how being here sits with one of your people, by race and zone (a Forsaken *"Easy in the grey light of the Glades"* in Tirisfal and *"Keeps to the shadows here, among the living"* in Stormwind; a night elf watching Ashenvale's treeline for orc axes; a gnome glancing up the road to Gnomeregan; a long road abroad).
- **Three new statistics**, each with its own switch and tooltip: *Time at home / abroad* (*"33 hours at home, 6 days abroad"*), *Racial ability most used* (*"Stoneform, 41 times, 9 of them against poison or bleeding"*) and *Old enemies slain* (*"212 Dark Iron dwarves, 40 troggs"*).
- **More places that matter to each people**: the Thandol Span, Aerie Peak and the Hall of Explorers for dwarves; Stratholme, Andorhal and Deathknell for the Forsaken; Red Rocks for tauren; the Valley of Spirits and Zul'Farrak for trolls; Silithus and the Temple of the Moon for night elves; Stromgarde, the Ruins of Lordaeron and Stormwind Keep for humans; and more.

### How they fight

- **How your character fights, from your own lines in the combat log** (`Sources/Style.lua`, with Total RP 3 alone). Each group fight is read as it ends from what you did in it: healing others more than you struck makes it a healer's fight, taunts, sunders, growls, mauls and shield blocks a defender's, the rest a striker's. Auto TRP also counts how much of your damage is up close, from range or by spell, the school you deal most, how long your fights run and how many you have an hour, how often you strike first in a group fight, the spells you cut off (Kick, Pummel, Counterspell, Earth Shock), the curses, poisons and sicknesses you lift from others, the foes you take out of a fight (polymorph, sap, fear, sleep, roots), the kinds of creature you fight most (read once as each fight starts) and your weapon skills. Other players are never kept, only counts. The combat log handler makes two comparisons and is out for every line that isn't yours; in a fight it only adds to a few counters, and every list is capped.
- **Personality** gets a short paragraph on how you fight, each line only once the counts back it, four or five ways to say each: *"Fights from the back of a group, mending more than striking."*, *"Steps in front when a group runs into trouble."*, *"Reaches for fire first."*, *"Is quick to cut off a spellcaster mid-word."*, *"Is usually first into a fight."*, *"Lifts curses and sickness from others without being asked."*, *"Ends most fights quickly."*
- **Physical** says how you carry yourself in a fight (*"Stands square and low in a fight, hard to move."*, *"Hangs back in a fight, lips moving."*). **History**'s older record tells the role you've mostly taken (*"has spent most of their fights keeping others standing"*), the spells you've cut off, the afflictions you've lifted, the kind of foe you've fought most and the weapons you've trained as far as your years allow.
- **Company** names the part you play in the group you're in now, from its own fights (or, before there are any, the role you usually take): *"Sharing the road with a warrior and two mages, the group's healer."*
- **Mood**: cutting off three or more casters in one fight is a quiet satisfaction (*"cut off four spells in one fight"*).
- **Two new statistics**, each with its own switch and tooltip: **Role** (*"Healer, mostly"*, *"Defender, nearly always"*, *"Healer and spellcaster, by turns"*) and **Speciality** (*"Holy, 31 points"*, *", and 20 in Discipline"* when a second tree has ten or more). Both join Faction, Guild and Level in the same ordered block.

### Reputation, as people would see it

- **Standing kept over time** (new `Sources/Renown.lua`, `AutoTRPCharDB.renown`). Every gain and loss the game announces is kept by faction and by day, with what caused it: the quest just turned in (an Argent Dawn token turned in four times counts four times) or the creature just killed, and the land it happened in. The faction list is read out of combat, and each step up or down between standings is remembered. Also counted: guards (bruisers, grunts, sentinels and the like) killed and killed by, by town; honourable kills and deaths to the other side, by land, never by name. Bounded: 80 factions, six causes each, twelve days of history, sixteen steps, twelve places.
- **What's said of them.** History's people paragraph now carries up to three lines of hearsay, each built from one stored record and never an invented deed: a town where they've done many jobs (*"Mention him in Goldshire and the talk soon turns to the gnoll raids on Elwynn's border."*), a faction fallen out with and why (*"The Bloodsail Buccaneers have no love for him, after the Bloodsail Swashbucklers he's cut down in Stranglethorn Vale."*), one done good work for (*"The Argent Dawn speak well of him, mostly for turning in "Minion's Scourgestones" four times."*), one cooling lately (*"The goblins of Booty Bay are warier of him than they were."*), the guards of a town (*"The watch in Booty Bay still counts the two of its guards he's put down."*) and the war in one land (*"The Horde count four of their own lost to him in Ashenvale."*). Five ways of saying each, local and modest (`famed` joins the banned phrases).
- **Standing in the news**: a step down (*"fell out with Booty Bay"*, *"slipped to friendly with the Cenarion Circle"*), a run of losses (*"lost ground with the Bloodsail Buccaneers"*) or a great deal of work no new standing has told of yet (*"put in long days for the Argent Dawn"*).
- **Full title from real standing**: with no town to name, Revered or Exalted with a faction gives *Friend of*, *Helper of*, *Ally of*, *Trusted by* or *Sworn Friend of the Argent Dawn* (after the alignment title when there is one).
- **Falling out is felt**: a step down between standings leaves them ashamed (*"fell out with Booty Bay"*) and a little bitter; losing 250 standing with one faction in a session makes them uneasy (once an hour).
- **New statistic, Standing** (*"Exalted with 1, Revered with 3, Honored with 2, out of favour with 1"*), with its own switch and tooltip like the others; "out of favour" only counts factions Auto TRP saw them lose standing with.

### Dungeons and raids as stories

- **Every run is kept** (new `Sources/Delves.lua`, `AutoTRPCharDB.delves`, with Total RP 3 alone). Each time you go into a dungeon or raid Auto TRP keeps when you went in and came out, how big the group was and its classes (counted, never named; a Social Diary friend by name, if you allow it), the masters brought down in order (`ENCOUNTER_END`), every failed try at one (a wipe), the group's deaths (from the combat log, counted, never named) and your own, the rare and better pieces you received inside, how far you got and whether it was a full clear of the place's known masters (by wing, for the Scarlet Monastery, Dire Maul, Blackrock Spire and Stratholme). A release and run back, or a hearth out for repairs, is the same run; half an hour away ends it. The last 40 runs are kept, with totals for each place.
- **History tells a run as a small story**, in its own paragraph after the news: *"Last week Aldric went down into the Deadmines with Mirelle and three others; the five of them broke against Mr. Smite twice, losing two along the way before Edwin VanCleef fell and the place was clear, and Aldric came out with Cruel Barb."*, followed by a line on what the place is like (*"Under Moonbrook the Defias have carved out a whole harbour, and a warship sits half-built in it."*). Then the master that fought hardest (*"He's broken against Magmadar in the Molten Core three times, and it still stands."*), the place you keep going back to, your first full clear and a place left unfinished (*"Shadowfang Keep is unfinished business: the furthest he's got is Razorclaw the Butcher."*). What the story tells isn't told again in the news or among your finds.
- **Every classic dungeon and raid**, Ragefire Chasm to Naxxramas (26 places), has its masters listed in order and four or five lines on what it's like.
- **Lately** has a fresh run for three hours after it (*A Hard Run*, *A Clean Sweep*, *Turned Back*, *Out of the Deep*: *"Back from the Deadmines with Mirelle and three others after a rough go of it: Sneed threw the five of them back twice, and one fell to it."*), and **Marks** has *A Hard Run* for an hour after one with two wipes, three deaths in the group or a death of your own.
- **Two new statistics**, each with its own switch and tooltip: **Deepest run** (*"The Deadmines, all 6 masters"*, *"Blackrock Depths, 14 masters"*) and **Wipes** (*"5, 3 of them to Magmadar"*).
- It stays light: the new combat log handler returns at once outside a dungeon or raid, and inside it for anything but `UNIT_DIED`; the group is read only out of a fight; every API goes through `U.Call`.

### What they carry

- **The bags, read.** A new record (`Sources/Pack.lua`) looks in the bags after the game says they changed (never in a fight, at most every few seconds) and remembers each notable thing from the first time it's seen, and where: companion pets and mount items, toys and novelties (a list in `W.PACK.TOYS`), fishing poles, keys (the keyring too), things that start a job, letters and books, recipes not yet learned, and grey keepsakes kept for their own sake; also the biggest stacks, how full the bags are, a quiver, ammo pouch, soul, herb, enchanting, engineering or mining bag, and the hearthstone's town. A letter or book whose title could carry someone's name is skipped. Bounded: 120 things, 12 changes, 20 stacks.
- **Gained and lost.** Something new in the bags is news; something sold, auctioned, given in a trade, posted or thrown away after a while is a change, with how long it was carried. Something put in the bank isn't lost, and keeps its first-seen time when it comes back.
- **About: Physical** has a short paragraph of what they carry, at most three sentences and one of them about the bags themselves when there's something to say (*"Has carried Tattered Ribbon since Westfall. Keeps Cat Carrier (Siamese) near the top of his pack. Is carrying twenty Linen Bandages, more than anything else."*). Item names in their quality colour; no two sentences say "in their pack".
- **At first glance**: Marks *Something to Deliver*, *Heavy Pack*, *A Small Companion*, *Something Fun*, *A Keepsake*, *Well Stocked*; Lately *A New Companion*, *A New Toy*, *Something New*, *Let Go*, *Given Away*, *Sent Off*, *Thrown Out*, and *Long Carried* (*"Has carried a Westfall Stew recipe card for three weeks."*) when nothing fresher is.
- **Personality** picks up a habit from a stack kept well stocked for three days or more (*"Never seems to travel without a few Linen Bandages to spare."*, potions, food, drink, arrows, reagents, trade goods), or from a pack of old keepsakes. **History** tells the thing kept longest (*"Has kept Tattered Ribbon since Westfall, three weeks now"*).
- **A new statistic, Treasured keepsake** (*"Tattered Ribbon, carried for three weeks, from Westfall"*), with its own switch and tooltip.
### The road, land by land

- **Auto TRP keeps a record of every land** (`Sources/Road.lua`, from game events alone): the time spent there (played time, with time away from the keyboard and in fights left out, and the days a stay lasted), the level on arrival and the levels gained there, the jobs turned in there and the storyline most of them belonged to, deaths and close calls there, the dungeons entered from it and the masters brought down in them, the subzones, towns and inns used there, its flight masters, the first and last visit and how often they've come back. A light ticker every thirty seconds does a few sums and nothing in a fight; sixty lands at most, the least visited dropped first.
- **History tells the road.** A new paragraph after the roots tells the three or four lands that mattered most, in the order reached, with what happened there: *"No land kept him longer than Westfall: the better part of a week, climbing from level ten to sixteen, mostly on the Defias trouble; it's also where Aldric fell twice. The Deadmines is close by, and it was from there that he went after Edwin VanCleef."* Then the land left almost at once (*"Left the Wetlands almost as soon as he arrived."*), the one they keep coming back to (*"Stormwind City keeps drawing him back; he's returned five times."*) and where they've been this past week, or that they're back somewhere after a long while (*"Is in Westfall again, for the first time in a month."*). Every fact is from the record; four to six ways of saying each part, chosen so a paragraph doesn't repeat itself, and a short note on what a land is (*"Westfall, the dry farmland west of Stormwind"*) where there's room. The road is the one place History says where they fell.
- **Lately**: *Back Again* (*"Back in Westfall after a month away."*) and *Moving On* (*"After the better part of a week in Westfall, has moved on to Duskwood."*).
- **A new statistic, Longest stay** (*"Westfall, 18 hours over 2 visits"*), with its own switch and tooltip.

### The work they take on

- **Every job sorted by kind.** A new record (`Sources/Work.lua`) sorts every job, when it's taken and when it's handed in, into the kind of work it is: slaying, gathering, courier work, escorts, scouting, rescues, bounties, dungeon work, jobs for your own class or trade, and festival errands. The quest log's objective types, header and tag decide it in any language; the English text tells escorts, rescues, bounties and errands apart. It also keeps who asked (NPCs only), where, the reward picked when there's a choice (kit or coin), jobs a companion shared (counted, never named), escorts seen through and lost, and jobs done again (counted apart). Nothing is read in a fight; every list is capped.
- **Work in the profile.** History tells what most of the work has been and where the next kind was done (*"Most of his work has been the killing kind, though he's carried more letters across Westfall than anyone needs to."*), the escorts (*"Has seen four escorts home safely and lost one on the road out of Darkshore."*), escorts in the news, and the quest-giver worked for most (*"Does a lot of work for Marshal Dughan."*, not repeated when he's already named as a friend). Personality picks up the habits the counts back (couriers, bounty boards, scouting, escorts, coin over kit, the same steady work, a companion's jobs); Lately has *Seen Safely Through*, *Lost on the Road* and runs of the same work; a lost escort is a sorrow and one seen home is satisfying. A new *Work by kind* statistic, with its own switch (on by default).

### The journal

- **Much more of the day.** The journal now draws on everything Auto TRP records as it happens: fish caught and the best of the catch (*"Had a line out for a while; six came in, an Oily Blackmouth the prize"*), things made at a trade, books read (by title; letters never), the emote the day was full of (*"no shortage of waving"*), duels won and lost, battlegrounds (the outcome and the killing blows), a new mount, the pet by its own name (hungry, content again, fallen), joining, leaving or rising in a guild, a storyline moved along or seen through (*"one more step in the key to Scholomance"*), a standing earned, jobs given up, a loot roll lost (the winner is never named), being healed by others (counted), a face met before (Social Diary friends by name, everyone else only counted), a day that ran for hours, and what's within reach (the next step up, or the next standing with a faction).
- **Written like a day's end.** An entry opens where you woke (an inn says so) and when you set out, keeps the road in one paragraph, the work of the day in the next, company and fortune after it, and closes on where you stopped, how you felt and the purse, a line when one feeling held all day, and a last thought in your own voice: by how the day went, by your temperament (RPG Dialogue's, or Auto TRP's own reading of how you play), and by your race, a Forsaken's dry *"Nothing went wrong, which is suspicious, but I'll allow it"*, a dwarf's *"Bah. Tomorrow, then"*, a night elf's *"One good day among very many, and I'll still remember it"*. Every beat has six or more ways to say it, and none of it invents an event.
- **Days to mark.** The first time at every tenth level (60 most of all), a first dungeon, a first death and a first mount open the day on it (*"Today I rode my first mount, my Brown Horse."*). Firsts other than levels are only claimed for a character Auto TRP has known from level 5 or under.
- **The week behind.** On the first session of a new week the journal looks back on the one before, from the days it kept: where you mostly were, the new ground, what it added up to (masters brought down, levels, a mount, a standing, the best find, jobs, battles, duels, fish, books, deaths) and how the days felt.
- **The journal window** shows each date in full (*"Thursday, 26 November"*), a marker for each week with its look back under it, and each day's mood with its icon.
- Kept bounded: ten kinds of fish, crafts and emotes a day, 150 events a day, 30 days of entries, six weekly look backs. Nothing is composed in combat.
- Fixed: the journal read a craft's new rank only half way.

### What they've fought

- **The bestiary: what they've fought and seen.** A new record (`Sources/Bestiary.lua`) notes what each creature you target or fight is: its kind (beast, humanoid, undead, demon, elemental, dragonkin, giant, machine), its family (wolf, spider, raptor...), how rare or strong the game calls it and its level against yours. Kills are counted by kind and family, with the first of each kind, the greatest thing you've brought down, the rares and elites you saw and left be, world bosses seen in the open, the kind that has killed you most, and the leaders and named creatures you've met. Other players and their pets are never taken in; a few reads at a target change or a fight's start, nothing looped in combat, and the record is bounded (80 creatures, 40 families, 30 sightings).
- **History** tells what they've fought in the older record (*"Has fought mostly wild beasts, wolves above all, and humanoids so far; the undead in Duskwood since. Of everything he's felled, Mor'Ladim stands out, a rare elite undead creature, far beyond him at the time."*), named creatures brought down, leaders met in person and world bosses seen. Firsts are only claimed when the record began near level one.
- **Personality** from the counts: a kind hunted far more than any other (*"Has put more of the undead back in the ground than most"*), a kind that has killed you three times or more (*"Is uneasy around demons, having died to them four times"*), rares chased down or let pass, elites taken on often.
- **Lately**: *Left Be* (*"Saw a rare spider in Duskwood and left it be."*), *A Sight to See* (a world boss), *Greatest Kill*, *A First*, *A Dragon*, *Met in Person*. **Mood**: a world boss in the open or a first dragon brings wonder; a rare spotted, a moment's intrigue.
- **Two statistics**: *Foes by kind* (*"412 beasts, 230 humanoids, 41 undead"*) and *Greatest kill* (*"Mor'Ladim, rare elite, level 35 (slain at 31)"*), each with its own switch and tooltip.

### The war

- **The war, from the game alone.** A new record (`Sources/War.lua`) of your part in the war between the factions. For Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley each: the flags you carry home and return, the bases you assault and defend, the graveyards and towers you take (from the battleground's own lines, only those that start with your own name, and your own row of the end scoreboard), how many bases your side held at the end and the time you spend there; games fought and won per battleground are now kept by Life's battle record. Your honour rank and its changes, the highest you've held, and your honourable kills (lifetime and today). In the open world: time flagged for the war, the contested lands you fight in, the enemy players you bring down and who bring you down, by class and race (counted, never named, looked up only after the fight), ganks (an enemy ten or more levels above you), and the enemies you bring down in your own faction's towns.
- **History** tells it (*"Has fought in Warsong Gulch thirty times and carried the flag home three times."*, *"Out in the open lands he's brought down twelve of the Horde's fighters and fallen to them four times."*, *"Carries the rank of Sergeant in the Alliance's army, and has stood as high as Knight."*), with news of a new or lost rank, a town defended and what you did in the last battle (*"won a battle in Warsong Gulch, carrying the flag home twice"*). The rank line now has five ways to say it, in your own faction's words.
- **Lately** says what happened in the battle (*"Back from Arathi Basin, where the Alliance held four bases."*, *"Back from Warsong Gulch, having carried the flag home twice."*), and has *Promoted* and *Town Defended*. **Marks** has *Fresh From Battle* for a quarter of an hour after one. **Company** in a battleground adds what you've done in this one, or how well you know it (*"Has fought here twelve times before."*, *"New to Alterac Valley."*).
- **Personality** gains habits from the record: going about always flagged, or seldom, defending your own towns, seeking out the enemy, going for the flag, taking bases or holding them, watching your back after ganks.
- **The mood**: a flag carried home is a triumph, a flag returned or a base held a quiet pride, a base or graveyard taken fierce, a town defended righteous, a gank humbling.
- **Two new statistics**, each with its own switch and tooltip: *Battlegrounds* (*"Warsong Gulch: 30 fought, 18 won, 3 flags captured, 1 returned; Arathi Basin: 12 fought, 7 won"*) and *Honour* (*"Sergeant, at best Knight; 412 honourable kills, 7 today; 12 brought down in the open, most of them rogues; 6 towns defended"*).
- Light by design: the combat log handler looks up its sub-event in a small table and leaves at once on anything else; kills are queued by GUID in memory and looked up after the fight; every list is capped (twelve classes and races, thirty lands and towns, ten rank changes).

### Trades in depth

- **A record of your trades** (new: `Sources/Trade.lua`, `AutoTRPCharDB.trade`), beside Life's counts of things made and gathered, from game events alone. ### The About page, edited

- **Personality, History and Physical read as one piece.** When two sentences in a row open on the same word, the second gets its subject back with verbs that agree (*"They hold"*, *"She carries"*); a one-sentence paragraph joins the one before. History runs in one order: roots, the road, the longer record, the news and the run stories, the people, their standing, and how things stand with what drives them. Personality merges beliefs with the inner life; Physical splits what's worn from what's carried when it runs long.
- **History keeps to a length.** Past about 2,300 letters the least of it gives way, a line at a time: the asides on how things stand (the journal or diary line stays), the runs, the older record, the people and their standing. Where it began, the news and what drives them always stay.
- **Said once.** A run told as a story isn't told again as news or in the older record; *"helped bring down A and B"* once; peak gold isn't told when it's what they hold now; a race's own homelands aren't *"a journey"*; *"helped bring down Edwin VanCleef and ten other named foes"* no longer muddles the list it joins.
- **Glances, statistics and the journal.** Style's role is its own short sentence in Company, and a glance drops its tail past its length; Lately fills every pronoun; *"the Deadmines's smoke"* fixed; statistics read *"12, all of them Defias Bandits"*, *"Revered with 1 faction"*, festivals without a bare count, and work by kind agrees with its number (*"1 escort"*, *"6 bounties"*); the journal keeps its subjects after *"and"*.

### Lately: how your character feels

- **The mood reads the game itself, with no other addon needed.** Without RPG Dialogue, Social Diary or Chronicle the mood used to have little to go on; now dozens of the game's own signals move it, and race, class and (when it's there) Auto TRP's own reading of your temperament bend each one:
  - **Other people**: someone waving, smiling at, hugging or thanking you lifts you (**cheerful**, **grateful**); spitting, rudeness or a laugh at your expense sours you (**bitter**, **humbled**). Being healed or blessed by someone else, or offered a resurrection, leaves you **grateful** (each helper counts once in half an hour, so a dungeon healer isn't a new kindness every spell). Strangers are never named or kept; Social Diary friends are (*"Mirelle hugged them"*).
  - **Fights**: three telling blows in a row make you **smug**; bringing down something far above you, **proud**; a run of easy grey kills, **bored**; running for it on low health, **humbled**; being polymorphed, feared, stunned or rooted moves you by kind; dying to an enemy player in the open world leaves you **bitter** (less so on a battlefield). Honourable kills are a quiet pride. The combat log only ticks a few counters and bails out at once for anything not about you; the feelings land after the fight.
  - **Fortune and failure**: a loot roll won is smug, one you needed and lost is bitter (no winner named); a job failed or given up stings. A duel won is smug, a duel lost humbling, and the other duellist is no longer named unless they're a friend.
  - **The body and the road**: a long fall survived rattles you, holding your breath to the last or swimming into deep water shakes you, three hours on the go leave you **exhausted** (more in the small hours), half an hour away from the keyboard or an hour with nothing happening makes you **bored**, two hours of play away from your hearth makes you **homesick**, and full rested energy is **cheerful**.
  - **Milestones**: a level after a long climb (three hours or more of play), a new mount the first time you ride it, a new rank in a craft (75, 150, 225, 300), a visit to the trainer (one feeling, however many lessons), coming back after three days or more away (*"back on the road after five days"*), a new land after dark, and a hunter's pet going hungry or content again.
  - **Seasons**: Winter Veil and Midsummer cheer, the Lunar Festival and lone Winter Veil nights bring homesickness, Love is in the Air is lonely on your own, Hallow's End nights are eerie.
- **Eight new moods**: Grateful, Humbled, Bored, Homesick, Smug, Exhausted, Cheerful and Bitter, each with six ways to say it in Currently, six or seven Mood glance lines (*"Has the softened face of someone a stranger just looked after."*, *"Smirks, then pretends not to."*), a line for every race and several classes (*"Has the look of a dwarf adding a name to an old grudge list."*), a badge icon and colour, and walk-ups (grateful, cheerful, smug and bored are open; bitter and exhausted aren't).

### Along the way, and what changed, with all of this

- **Month by month, fitted into the new History.** The dated moments of 0.21.0 now have their own paragraph between the longer record and the news, and tell only what's older than the news (the last three weeks): a first dungeon or raid, a craft's new rank or a first battleground won are told in the news while they're fresh (*"won his first battleground, in Warsong Gulch"* in place of the plain battle line).
