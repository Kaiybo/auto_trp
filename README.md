# Auto TRP

For World of Warcraft: Forever, and a companion to [RPG Dialogue](https://github.com/Kaiybo/rpg-dialogue). It keeps your **Total RP 3** profile in step with your character's story: what they feel, where they are, what they believe, who they know and what they've been through.

**Everything works with Total RP 3 alone.** Auto TRP reads your character from what you actually do in the game: who you heal and resurrect (strangers count most), what you pick fights with, the gifts you hand over in trades and the auctions you post, your prayers and holy work, how often you speak up, dance or group, the quests you see through or give up on, the towns you rest and work in, the innkeepers, quest-givers and trainers you keep going back to, the books you read and the storylines your quests follow. From that come your alignment and trait bars, Personality, standing in towns (*"Is liked in Goldshire"*), the people in your History (NPCs only: other players are never named), your stories and the Full title (*Lightsworn, Friend of Goldshire*). It counts, it never reads what you say, and it never keeps who you healed or traded with. It also keeps its own record of the quests you turn in, the dungeon and raid masters you bring down, deaths, lands, kills and flights.

Three other addons add more if you have them (all optional, and Auto TRP never changes them). Where one has something to say, its reading comes first and Auto TRP's own fills the gaps:

- **RPG Dialogue**: your origin, alignment and traits, opinions you've voiced, standing in towns, the people you've met, the stories you've followed, your diary.
- **Social Diary**: the players you've marked as friends (and the relationship you wrote down for them), your recent groups.
- **Chronicle** (AzerothForge Adventure Chronicle): your goals, bosses brought down, dungeons, rare beasts, professions, deaths, and its equipment and stat snapshots for your appearance. Your private Chronicle notes are never read.

## What it writes

**It reacts as things happen.** Over two dozen game events (changing zone or subzone, resting, leaving combat, a buff or sickness landing, mounting, shapeshifting, your pet, joining a group, the mailbox, bank or crafting table, loot, money, your emotes, a death, a level, a boss, a conversation in RPG Dialogue, new gear, reputation) trigger an update within a couple of seconds. **Currently** and the glances are rewritten whenever they change (at most every 15 seconds), the story within half a minute of something that matters, and anything held back during combat is written the moment it ends.

**It stays light.** Nothing is worked out during combat. The other addons' data is read only when it changes (a glance at a few counters every five seconds), reputations, skills and gear only when the game says they changed, and a burst of events makes one update, not many.

## Life on the road

Auto TRP keeps its own record of the rest of your character's life, from the game alone (no other addon needed), and the profile draws on it:

- **Fishing, crafts, gathering**: every fish caught (by kind and where), everything made at a trade ("You create"), herbs, ore and hides gathered.
- **Duels and battles**: duels won and lost, battlegrounds fought and won, killing blows on the enemy. Opponents are never kept by name.
- **Money**: the most gold ever held, coin looted, paid for jobs, spent at vendors, on repairs, on training and on flights, and auctions sold.
- **The road**: the inns you rest at, every hearthstone home, the flight masters you find, each mount the first time you ride it, every level reached (and how long the last one took, when the game says through `/played`; Auto TRP never asks itself, since asking prints to chat).
- **Habits and company**: the emotes you use most, how many different people you've grouped with (counted, never named), times brought back by someone else or by the spirit healer.
- **Hard knocks**: falls and drowning (a death from a fall is the fall's, not the last foe's), close calls (under a tenth of your health and still standing), the hardest hit dealt and taken.
- **Gear and reading**: the first rare and first epic piece you put on, the books you read (by title) and the letters you open (never who wrote them).

It shows up as news in History (*"pulled a Slitherskin Mackerel out of the water in Elwynn Forest"*, *"won a battle in Warsong Gulch"*, *"took to riding a Brown Horse"*, *"rose to Cleric of the Light in Duskwood, only nine days after the rank before"*), in the longer record (*"Between roads he's a journeyman herbalist, and so far he's caught more than a hundred fish in four different lands, cooked more than a hundred meals and picked more than a hundred herbs."*, *"won fourteen of seventeen duels, rested at seven different inns and had as much as 312 gold to his name at once"*), in Lately (*A Fresh Catch*, *Back From Battle*, *A New Mount*, *Fresh Kit*, *Reading*, *At Work*, *Duel Won*), in Marks (*Smells of Fish*, *Cooking Smoke*, *Rock Dust*, *Fresh From a Duel*, *Sore* after a fall, *Soaked* in the water), and as habits in Personality once the counts back them (*"Fishes whenever there's water nearby."*, *"Rarely turns down a duel, and mostly wins."*, *"Bows to people more than most do."*).

It stays light: the combat log handler looks up its sub-event in a small table and leaves at once on anything else, the group is read only out of a fight, and every list is capped.

## Trades in depth

Beside Life's counts, Auto TRP keeps a closer record of your trades (`Sources/Trade.lua`), from the game alone: how many recipes each trade knows (read from its window only when you open it, never in a fight), the recipes you learn over time, the finest thing you've ever made and the first of each notable piece, a specialisation (weaponsmith or armorsmith, gnomish or goblin engineer, dragonscale, elemental or tribal leatherworker, the transmutes an alchemist knows), the bandages you put on other people, the enchantments and locks you do for others through the trade window, the meals you cook in a group, the land you do most of your gathering in, and the tools in your bags. Whoever you helped is only counted, never named.

It becomes a passage in History between roads (*"Knows 112 tailoring patterns and has made Robes of Arcana; is a gnomish engineer, for better or worse. Over time he's patched up other people twelve times and opened three lockboxes for whoever asked. Gathers his herbs mostly in Elwynn Forest."*), news (*"learned to make Runed Copper Rod"*, *"made his first Robes of Arcana"*), a habit in Personality (*"Is quick to bandage other people's wounds."*), Lately (*A New Recipe*, *Finest Work Yet*, *First of Its Kind*, *Enchanting for Others*, *A Lock Opened*, *Field Dressing*), Marks (the smell of a trade after a stretch at it, *Forge Soot* to *Arcane Dust*, and *Tools of the Trade*), and two statistics: *Recipes known* (*"154: 112 tailoring, 40 cooking, 2 enchanting"*) and *Finest work* (*"Robes of Arcana, rare, tailoring"*).

## Your calling

Auto TRP also keeps a record of the work only your class does, from your own spells and the game's own events (no other addon needed): a warrior's time in each stance, charges and intercepts, and Shield Walls and Last Stands raised on low health; a rogue's pockets picked (and what came out of them), locks picked, poisons put on a blade and Vanishes in time; a hunter's beasts tamed (by kind), tracking, traps and Feign Deaths that saved them; a mage's portals (and how many went through), teleports, conjured food and water handed over in trades, Polymorphs and Blinks out of trouble; a warlock's summonings, soulstones placed on others, demons called by kind and healthstones made and handed over; a priest's resurrections (strangers counted apart), Power Word: Shield and Fortitude on others and Mind Control; a shaman's totems by element, time as a Ghost Wolf, Reincarnations and Ancestral Spirits; a paladin's blessings on others, Redemption, Lay on Hands and Divine Intervention; a druid's time in each shape, Rebirths, Innervates and Thorns on others; a death knight's Death Grips, ghouls and presences. Other players are only ever counted.

It becomes habits in Personality, only when the counts back them (*"Has resurrected more strangers than most priests would bother to."*, *"Fights from a defensive stance by habit, as if someone always needs covering."*, *"Has brought five wild beasts to heel, cats and crabs among them."*), news and the longer record in History (*"tamed a cat in Duskwood"*, *"made the sacrifice of Divine Intervention"*, *"he's picked thirty locks and lifted what he could from sixty pockets"*), Lately (*A New Companion*, *Opened the Way*, *A Summoning*, *Raised the Fallen*, *A Sacrifice*, *Back From Death*, *A Narrow Escape*, *Bread and Water*), Marks (*Poison-Stained*, *Light Fingers*, *Freshly Conjured*, *Brimstone*, *Drawn and Tired*, *Hard Pressed*) and four statistics: Calling (*"60 pockets picked for 2 gold; 30 locks picked"*), Narrow escapes (*"6 by Vanish"*), Given to others (*"8 raised, all of them strangers; 120 shields"*) and Stances and forms (*"Defensive Stance 6 hours, Battle Stance 3 hours"*). A class without one of them (a rogue has no stances to count) has that entry left off.

## The work they take on

Every job is sorted, when it's taken and when it's handed in, into the kind of work it is: slaying, gathering, courier work, escorts, scouting, rescues, bounties, dungeon work, jobs for your own class or trade, and festival errands. The quest log's own objective types (creatures to kill, things to collect or use, events) and its header and tag decide it in any language; the English text of the job tells escorts, rescues, bounties and errands apart, and on another language it simply leaves them to the objective types. Auto TRP also keeps who asked (the quest-giver, never a player), where, the reward you pick when there's a choice (something to wear, or the one that sells best), jobs a companion shared (counted, never named), escorts seen through and lost, and jobs done again (counted apart, so a stack of Argent Dawn coins isn't a career).

It shows in History's longer record (*"Most of his work has been the killing kind, though he's carried more letters across Westfall than anyone needs to."*, *"Has seen four escorts home safely and lost one on the road out of Darkshore."*), in the news (*"saw an escort safely through Redridge Mountains"*), among the people (*"Does a lot of work for Marshal Dughan."*, unless he's already named there as a friend), in Personality once the counts back it (*"Takes every courier job going."*, *"Prefers coin to kit when a job pays out."*), in Lately (*Seen Safely Through*, *Lost on the Road*, a run of errands or hunting jobs), in the mood (an escort lost on the road is a sorrow; one seen home is satisfying), and in the *Work by kind* statistic (*40 slaying, 22 courier, 9 gathering, 5 escort; 1 escort lost*). Nothing is read in a fight, and every list is capped (30 quest-givers, 30 lands, 40 jobs on hand).

## How you fight

Auto TRP also keeps its own record of how your character fights (`Sources/Style.lua`), from your own lines in the combat log alone:

- **The role you take in a group**: each group fight is read as it ends, from what you did in it: healing others more than you struck (healer), taunts, sunders, growls, mauls and shield blocks (holding the foe), or striking. *Role* says what most of them were (*"Healer, mostly"*, *"Defender, nearly always"*, *"Healer and spellcaster, by turns"*).
- **How you strike**: up close, from range or with spells, and the school you deal most (Fire, Frost, Shadow, Holy, Nature, Arcane or Physical).
- **Habits**: how long your fights run and how many you have an hour, how often you strike first in a group fight, the spells you cut off (Kick, Pummel, Counterspell, Earth Shock), the curses, poisons and sicknesses you lift from others, the foes you take out of a fight (polymorph, sap, fear, sleep, roots), the kinds of creature you fight most (read once as each fight starts), and your weapon skills.

It shows up in Personality (*"Fights from the back of a group, mending more than striking."*, *"Reaches for fire first."*, *"Is quick to cut off a spellcaster mid-word."*, each only once the counts back it), in Physical (*"Stands square and low in a fight, hard to move."*), in History's older record (*"has spent most of their fights keeping others standing"*), in the Company glance (*"Travelling with a warrior and two mages, the group's healer."*, from the fights in the group you're in now) and in the mood: several casters cut off in one fight is a quiet satisfaction. Other players are never kept, only counts; the combat log handler makes two comparisons and is out for every line that isn't yours, and in a fight it only adds to a few counters.

## Dungeons and raids

Every run into a dungeon or raid is kept as a story (`Sources/Delves.lua`), from the game alone: when you went in and came out, the group's size and classes (counted, never named; a Social Diary friend by name, if you allow it), the masters brought down in order, every wipe and which master caused it, the group's deaths (counted from the combat log) and your own, the rare and better pieces you received inside, how far you got and whether you cleared the place (by wing, where it has wings). Stepping out to release and run back is the same run. The last 40 runs are kept, with totals for each place.

History tells the most telling recent run in a paragraph of its own (*"Last week Aldric went down into the Deadmines with Mirelle and three others; the five of them broke against Mr. Smite twice, losing two along the way before Edwin VanCleef fell and the place was clear, and Aldric came out with Cruel Barb."*), with a line on what the place is like (every classic dungeon and raid, Ragefire Chasm to Naxxramas, has four or five), the master that fought hardest, the place you keep going back to, your first full clear and a place left unfinished. Lately has a fresh run, Marks a hard one, and the statistics gain *Deepest run* (*"The Deadmines, all 6 masters"*) and *Wipes* (*"5, 3 of them to Magmadar"*). The combat log handler returns at once outside a dungeon, and inside it for anything but a death.

## What they carry

Auto TRP looks in the bags too (only after the game says they changed, never in a fight) and remembers each notable thing from the first time it sees it, and where: companion pets and mount items, toys and novelties, fishing poles, keys, things that start a job, letters and books (never one whose title could carry another player's name), recipes not yet learned and grey keepsakes kept for their own sake; also the biggest stacks, how full the bags are, a quiver, soul bag or herb bag, and the town the hearthstone is set to. Something sold, auctioned, given, posted or thrown away after a long while is news; something banked isn't lost.

It becomes a few sentences in About: Physical (*"Has carried Tattered Ribbon since Westfall. Keeps Cat Carrier (Siamese) near the top of his pack. Is carrying twenty Linen Bandages, more than anything else."*), Marks (*Something to Deliver*, *A Small Companion*, *Something Fun*, *A Keepsake*, *Heavy Pack*, *Well Stocked*), Lately (*A New Companion*, *Let Go*, *Long Carried*: *"Has carried a Westfall Stew recipe card for three weeks."*), a habit in Personality once a stack has been kept up for days (*"Never seems to travel without a few Linen Bandages to spare."*), the keepsake kept longest in History, and the *Treasured keepsake* statistic (*"Tattered Ribbon, carried for three weeks, from Westfall"*). Only what's in the bags is ever named.

## The road, land by land

Auto TRP also keeps a record of every land your character spends time in, from the game alone: how long (played time, leaving out time away from the keyboard and in fights, and how many days a stay lasted), the level they arrived at and the levels gained there, the jobs turned in there and the storyline most of them were part of, deaths and close calls there, the dungeons entered from it and the masters brought down inside, the subzones, towns and inns used, its flight masters, the first and last visit and how often they've come back.

History tells it in a paragraph of its own, after the roots: the three or four lands that mattered most, in the order reached (*"No land kept him longer than Westfall: the better part of a week, climbing from level ten to sixteen, mostly on the Defias trouble; it's also where Aldric fell twice."*), the land left almost at once, the place they keep coming back to and where they've been lately (*"Is in Westfall again, for the first time in a month."*). Lately can say *Back Again* or *Moving On*, and the *Longest stay* statistic names the land they've spent most time in. A ticker every thirty seconds does a few sums and nothing in a fight; at most sixty lands are kept, the least visited dropped first.

## Race and class

Your race and class shape almost everything: the rank ladder, how each mood shows on you (a tauren's ears pinned back, a Forsaken's creaking joints, a mage's sparks at the fingertips), what you get up to on the road (a hunter tracking, a shaman listening to the spirits, a warlock gathering reagents best not named), your background when RPG Dialogue has no origin for you, and your ambitions. Places mean something to your race and class (a night elf grieves in corrupted Felwood, a dwarf bristles in Dark Iron land, a Forsaken remembers the ruins of Lordaeron, a paladin finds peace at Light's Hope), and so do your spells and racial abilities (resurrecting a companion, opening a portal, Shadowmeld, Cannibalize, Blood Fury...). A hunter's pet's happiness shows too.

## Your people and your homeland

Auto TRP keeps a record of your character's bond to their people, from the game alone:

- **Home and abroad**: the time you play in your race's capital and home lands (Elwynn and the Stormwind lands, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil, Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal...) against the time abroad, and every homecoming, back in the capital after three days or more away (a homecoming lifts the mood too).
- **Racial gifts**: Stoneform, Escape Artist, Shadowmeld, Blood Fury, Berserking, War Stomp, Will of the Forsaken, Cannibalize, Perception, Find Treasure, counted, and noted when one mattered: Will of the Forsaken out of a fear, charm or sleep, Escape Artist out of a root or a snare, Stoneform against a poison or a bleed, Shadowmeld mid-fight, Blood Fury, Berserking or War Stomp with the fight going badly, Cannibalize to mend.
- **Tongues**: which language you speak aloud in (`/say` and `/yell`), counted by language from the game's chat event, never by the words.
- **Places and old enemies**: the places that matter to your people (more of them now: the Thandol Span, Stratholme, Red Rocks, the Valley of Spirits...), and the creatures you kill that are your people's old enemies, by the names the game gives them (the Scarlet Crusade and the Scourge for the Forsaken, satyrs for night elves, Dark Irons for dwarves, centaurs and the Grimtotem for tauren...). Your home city's standing is read outside a fight.

It shows up as news in History (*"came home to Ironforge after twelve days away"*, *"made the journey to Uldaman"*, *"threw off a fear with the will of the Forsaken"*), in the longer record (*"gone home to Thunder Bluff three times after long roads, killed hundreds of centaurs"*), in Lately (*Home Again*, a place of your people by its feeling, a gift that mattered), in Marks (*Fed on the Fallen*, *Stone-Skinned*, *Blood Fury*, *Berserk*), in Personality once the record backs it (*"Hasn't set foot in Ironforge in over a month."*, *"Rarely strays far from Mulgore."*, *"Has a long quarrel with the Scarlet Crusade, and keeps it up."*), in Physical (*"Says most of what he says aloud in Dwarvish."*, and how being here sits with one of your people: *"Easy in the grey light of the Glades."* for a Forsaken in Tirisfal, *"Keeps to the shadows here, among the living."* in Stormwind), and in three statistics: *Time at home / abroad* (*"33 hours at home, 6 days abroad"*), *Racial ability most used* (*"Stoneform, 41 times, 9 of them against poison or bleeding"*) and *Old enemies slain* (*"212 Dark Iron dwarves, 40 troggs"*).

## How your character feels

Dozens of signals feed it, most of them from the game itself (no other addon needed), and each feeling remembers why (`/atrp mood` shows the reasons):

- **Death**: shaken right after, then **downcast** for hours; die again soon and you're **frustrated**. Resurrection sickness leaves you **drained**. Watching a party member fall brings sorrow and anger.
- **Blessings and ailments**: three or more buffs and you're **in fine fettle**; a world buff makes you **glorious**; Well Fed, **well-fed**. Disease, poison or a curse leaves you **ailing**; low health out of a fight, **wounded**.
- **Fortune**: a rare or epic drop **thrills**, a windfall makes you **flush**, an empty purse **worried**, full bags **overloaded**, broken gear frustrating. Almost at the next level: **eager**.
- **Deeds**: a level **elates**, a boss is a **triumph**, a run of fights makes you **battle-hungry**, a run of finished jobs **accomplished**, a close call **rattled**.
- **Places**: **at ease** in an inn or sanctuary, **on guard** in a dungeon or enemy land, **uneasy** in Duskwood or the Plaguelands at night, **wide-eyed** somewhere new, **glad to be home**, **festive** at Winter Veil, Hallow's End, Midsummer, Brewfest and the rest.
- **Company**: in good company in a group, happier with Social Diary friends, **solitary** after a long time alone.
- **Your story** (RPG Dialogue): a dark choice leaves you **conflicted**, a good one **righteous**; goodwill earned makes you **proud**, lost **ashamed**; a story beat **intrigues**; a letter brings **news**; drink makes you **merry**; madness **haunts**. A Chronicle goal reached **elates**.
- **Your emotes**: /cry, /dance, /pray, /flirt, /rage, /cower, /wave and more move your mood.
- **Other people**: a wave, smile or hug aimed at you **cheers** you; thanks, a heal, a blessing or an offered resurrection from someone else makes you **grateful**; being spat at or laughed at leaves you **bitter** or **humbled**. Strangers are never named; Social Diary friends are.
- **Fights and contests**: a run of telling blows makes you **smug**, killing something far above you **proud**, a string of easy kills **bored**; running for it on low health, being polymorphed, feared or stunned, or losing a duel **humbles**; dying to an enemy player leaves you **bitter**. A loot roll won is smug, one lost after you needed it bitter; a job failed or abandoned stings.
- **The body and the clock**: a long fall rattles you; nearly drowning shakes you; three hours on the go (or the small hours) leaves you **exhausted**; idling or nothing happening makes you **bored**; hours away from your hearth make you **homesick**; full rested energy, a new mount, early light and coming back after days away make you **cheerful**. A level after a long climb, a new craft rank and a trainer visit are their own small prides.
- **Temperament** bends all of it: brave characters shake less, kind ones grieve more and are more grateful, greedy ones love a windfall and hate a lost roll, the devout take prayer to heart. Race and class bend it too (a goblin is smug about a won roll, the Forsaken don't get homesick), so it works with no other addon installed.

`/atrp feel <mood>` sets it yourself for an hour.

**The mood badge**: a small icon on screen shows your current mood. Hover for the reasons, what your profile says and when it was last written; click for the preview, shift-click to see your Currently in your own chat, ctrl-click for the settings, right-click to pause, drag to move (`/atrp badge` hides it). It glows briefly when your mood turns. When your mood turns you get a quiet line in your own chat (never sent to anyone).

**The mood journal** keeps each day's main feeling and place for two weeks. History sums up the last few days (*"These past days: downcast in Duskwood today and wide-eyed in Westfall yesterday."*), and `/atrp days` lists the week.

## The journal

Auto TRP keeps a journal for your character, written in the first person from what actually happened each day you play. It needs nothing but the game: it listens for the lands you enter (and whether it's your first time), the jobs you turn in and the storylines they move along, jobs given up, dungeons and the masters you bring down, battlegrounds and how they ended, levels, deaths and close calls (and what did it, never a player's name), rare and epic finds and rolls lost, the coin you came out ahead or behind, the groups you joined (counted; only your Social Diary friends are named, and only if you let Auto TRP name them, and faces you've travelled with before are only counted), being healed by others, inns, flights, crafts and skills, fish (and the best of the catch), books read, duels, a new mount, your pet (by its name), your guild, a standing earned, the emote the day was full of, and a long day. A day to mark (every tenth level, a first dungeon, death or mount) opens on it.

Entries read like someone writing at day's end: where you woke and when you set out, the road, the work of the day, the company and fortune, then where you stopped, how you felt, and a last thought in your own voice. That voice follows how the day went, your temperament (RPG Dialogue's, or Auto TRP's own reading of how you play) and your race: a Forsaken's dry wit, a dwarf's bluster, a night elf's long view, all in word choice, never in made-up events.

> Today I rode my first mount, my Brown Horse. Woke at the Lion's Pride Inn and was away before the sun was well up, turned in "Araj's Scarab" before I left, and another piece of the key to Scholomance done. Joined the fighting at Warsong Gulch with Mirelle and one other, new to me. We came out on top, and three of the enemy fell to my hand. Went back to Elwynn Forest.
>
> Had a line out for a while; six came in, an Oily Blackmouth the prize. Got five pieces of first aid done, Linen Bandage the most of them, and read "The Birth of the Lich King". Came out of three duels with two won and one lost, and no shortage of waving.
>
> Among the company was a face I'd travelled with before, and two people saw to my hurts along the way. Lost the roll for Thief's Blade, and turned up Thornstone Sledgehammer. Felt myself grow stronger, and I'm honoured with Stormwind now. Took my place in Knights of the Vale, and Grimfang came round to a good mood. Some hours on my feet, all told. Another step up isn't far off.
>
> Took a room at the Lion's Pride Inn, in good cheer. The cheer lasted from start to finish. A good day, plain and simple; there's no better kind.

On the first session of a new week it also looks back on the week before: where you mostly were, the new ground, what it added up to and how the days felt.

The day's entry is written when you log out (and whenever you open it), from that day's record and nothing else, and the last 30 days are kept (and six weekly look backs). `/atrp journal` opens it, each day under its date and its mood (with the mood's icon) and each week under its own marker; `/atrp journal today` prints today's in your chat. Nothing is worked out in combat.

On your profile: when RPG Dialogue has no diary entry for you, History's last paragraph quotes the first sentence or two of your latest entry (deaths and near misses stay out, as they do everywhere in History), and the *Lately* glance quotes it when nothing fresher is going on. The setting "Quote your Auto TRP journal..." turns that off; the journal itself is always kept.

## Only what the game backs up

Auto TRP can draw conclusions from what the game records (a kind character from kind choices, a broad build from high strength, "gets by" from your coin against your level), but it never invents what your character did, holds or went through. Mood lines describe a face, a posture or a voice, not an action you weren't doing (no sitting under trees, reading scripture or polishing a helm), and there are no made-up backstories or "the road has made them..." lines.

The profile never describes something your character doesn't have. Gear is named only when it's equipped, and by its own name (*"Wears Crown of the Lost and Robe of the Moccasin"*), never "a hat" or "a holy symbol" from thin air. A mood line that mentions a weapon, helm, shield or pet is used only when you have one, and names the real one (*"Leaning on his Staff of Westfall more than usual"*). Your class's reagents (Soul Shards, totems, Arcane Powder, Sacred Candles...) are mentioned only when they're in your bags, and a coin pouch only when you carry coin (how full it is follows your wealth). How worn your gear looks follows its real durability and names the worst piece (*"Their Robe of the Moccasin shows hard use"*, *"...is broken outright and needs a smith"*). Looks are limited to what every one of your race shares, with nothing you can customise, like beards, hair or skin colour.

## Not too famous

A profile is public, so the character isn't made out to be widely known. What's said of them is hearsay from one town, one faction's people or one town's guards, and only ever about something Auto TRP saw happen (a quest turned in, a creature or guard killed, standing lost or gained). Standing is local and modest (*"is liked in Goldshire"*, *"is trusted in Auberdine"*, never "a hero to"), the full title is at most *"Friend of Goldshire"*, reputations read *"Has done good work for Stormwind"*, and rank titles stay clear of offices and patrons (no Thane, Warlord, Magister, SI:7 Agent or Archbishop's Chosen). `tests/banned_phrases.txt` holds the line.

## Your words stay yours

- Auto TRP writes to **one profile**. On first login it offers to make a copy of your current profile called *"Name (Auto TRP)"* and only ever writes there. Switch to another profile and it stops.
- It never writes to Total RP 3's default profile (Total RP 3 doesn't allow it).
- **Switched off means taken down.** Turn off a statistic and Auto TRP removes what it wrote there; turn off a glance and it's hidden (Total RP 3 keeps the text). Other fields simply stop updating. Anything you've edited is left as it is.
- **It never writes over your own words.** If a field already has text it didn't write, or you edit something it wrote, that field becomes yours and it stops touching it. `/atrp claim <field>` hands one back.
- Nothing is written in combat. *Currently* and the glances update at most every 90 seconds; the rest every five minutes or after something that matters.
- **Backup**: before its first write to a profile, Auto TRP keeps a copy of everything it might touch. `/atrp restore` puts the profile back exactly and hands every field back to you.
- Profile text is public, so it never gives your exact position, never reads Chronicle notes, and only names other players you've marked as friends in Social Diary (switch that off in settings). Everyone else in your group is only counted: by class, race and guild, and familiar faces by a scrambled mark of their character ID, so Auto TRP never stores who they were.
- Optional: pause *Currently* and the glances while you're out of character.

## Install

Copy the `AutoTRP` folder into `World of Warcraft/<your Forever folder>/Interface/AddOns/` and restart the game. Needs **Total RP 3**.

## Getting at it

- **Welcome**: on first login a small window says what Auto TRP does and offers to make its profile, use your current one, open the settings, or stop asking. `/atrp welcome` brings it back.
- **Settings**: `/atrp`, or Options > AddOns > Auto TRP. At the top, which profile it writes to and what each addon adds (Total RP 3 alone is enough; RPG Dialogue, Social Diary and Chronicle add more). Every switch explains itself when you hover over it, each heading has its own **On** / **Off**, and **All on** / **All off** do everything at once. Along the bottom: Preview, Write it now, Journal, Restore (asks first) and Make a profile for it.
- **The preview** (`/atrp preview`, or click the badge): tabs for *Now* (mood, Currently and the glances with their icons), *About*, *Names and titles* and *Statistics*. Each field says whether it's Auto TRP's, yours (left alone), switched off or not written yet, and has a box on the right holding its text: click it, then Ctrl+C. Refresh and Write it now are at the bottom. Drag to move, drag the corner to resize; it remembers where you left it, and Escape closes it.
- **The mood badge** on screen: hover for your mood and why, your Currently, when the profile was last written and what changed. Click for the preview, shift-click to see your Currently in your own chat, ctrl-click for the settings, right-click to pause, drag to move. It glows briefly when your mood turns.
- **The AddOns menu** by the minimap: click Auto TRP for the preview, ctrl-click for the settings, right-click to pause or resume.
- **Key bindings** (Key Bindings > AddOns > Auto TRP): show the preview, pause or resume, write my profile now.
- `/atrp help` lists every command below.

## Language

Auto TRP writes in English. Zones are recognised by their map ID, so zone touches (atmosphere, eerie places, places that matter to your race and class, capitals, flights) work in any game language. City districts, a few subzone places and dungeon descriptions are matched by their English names and are skipped on other languages. Everything else works the same.

## For developers

- `lua5.1 tests/run.lua` runs an offline test against a mock of the game, Total RP 3 and sample data from all three addons, and prints the profile it writes.
- Releasing: bump `## Version` in `AutoTRP/AutoTRP.toc` and add a matching `## <version> (date): Title` section to `CHANGELOG.md` in your pull request. When it merges into `main`, the release workflow tests, builds `AutoTRP-<version>.zip` and publishes the release with that changelog section as its notes. A merge that doesn't change the version releases nothing.
- `TRP.ORDER` (TRP.lua) is the order fields are written in, so new entries land the same way every time; statistics keep the order of `Compose.STATS`, and `tidyStats` gathers Auto TRP's own into one ordered run.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Calling.lua` is the record of each class's signature work (spells sorted once by ID through `U.Call(GetSpellInfo)`, the combat log only for the player's own buffs and resurrections on another player), kept in `AutoTRPCharDB.calling` (counters, form times, 12 beasts, the 40 latest notable acts) and read into `ns.cache.calling`. It also writes its own lines (`Calling.Habits`, `Events`, `Older`, `Lately`, `Marks`, `Stats`, called from Compose) from `W.CALLING` in `AutoTRP/Data/Calling.lua`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Work.lua` is Auto TRP's record of the work the character takes on (each job's kind, who asked, rewards picked, escorts), read into `ns.cache.work`; its wording is in `W.WORK`, and Compose calls `Work.News`, `Work.Record`, `Work.GiverLine`, `Work.Habits`, `Work.Lately` and `Work.Stat`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Bestiary.lua` is Auto TRP's bestiary: when you target a creature or a fight starts it reads the creature's kind, family, classification and level (other players and their pets are skipped), then counts kills by kind and family (joined with `Sources/Deeds.lua`'s kills by name), the first of each kind, the greatest kill, rares and elites seen but let be, world bosses seen, the kind that has killed you most and the notable figures met (`W.BESTIARY.NOTABLE`). Read into `ns.cache.bestiary`; its wording is in `W.BESTIARY` and the `bst_` and `lately_` keys of `W.SAY`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Life.lua` is Auto TRP's record of life on the road (fishing, crafts, duels, battles, money, inns, flight masters, mounts, levels, emotes, company, close calls, hits, gear, books), read into `ns.cache.life`; its wording is in `W.LIFE` and the `ev_`, `lately_`, `mark_` keys of `W.SAY`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Kin.lua` is the record of a character's people (`AutoTRPCharDB.kin`: time at home and abroad, homecomings, racial gifts and when they mattered, languages spoken, places of their people, old enemies killed, the home city's standing), read into `ns.cache.kin`; it also gives Compose the pieces it writes with (`Kin.Lately`, `Kin.Marks`, `Kin.Bearing`, `Kin.Events`, `Kin.Older`, `Kin.Voice`, `Kin.Here`, `Kin.Stats`). Its wording is `W.KIN` in `AutoTRP/Data/Kin.lua`, which also adds places to `W.RACE_PLACES`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Pack.lua` reads the bags (lazily: `BAG_UPDATE_DELAYED`, at most every few seconds, never in combat) into `AutoTRPCharDB.pack` (notable things by first sight, capped at 120; things gained and lost, the last 12; stacks kept well stocked, 20) and `ns.cache.pack`. `AutoTRP/Keepsakes.lua` words it from `W.PACK` (Physical's carry paragraph, Marks, Lately, habits, History and the keepsake statistic), through `Compose.Keepsakes*` call sites.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Road.lua` is Auto TRP's record of the road, land by land (`AutoTRPCharDB.road`: time, levels, deaths, close calls, dungeons, masters, subzones, towns, inns, flight masters, visits), read into `ns.cache.road` with the jobs done in each land from `Sources/Deeds.lua`. `Compose.RoadStory` tells it in History, `Compose.RoadLately` in Lately; the wording is `W.ROAD` in `Words.lua` and `W.ZONE_KIND` (what each land is) in `Places.lua`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Company.lua` keeps the company record (`AutoTRPCharDB.company`): outings, companions as salted GUID hashes, pets first seen and fallen, the guild first seen, time grouped and alone, dungeons entered alone. Its wording is `W.COMPANY` in `Words.lua`.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Goals.lua` reads the quest log, skill caps and reputation bars (lazily: again only after the game says they changed, never in combat) and ranks the ambitions (`Goals.Gather`, scores in `Goals.SCORE`); `Compose.Ambitions` adds the Chronicle goal and RPG Dialogue story, and `Compose.AmbitionGlance` and `Compose.Drives` word them from `W.GOALS` and `W.DRIVES`.
- `AutoTRP/Journal.lua` keeps the journal (`AutoTRPCharDB.journal`: a few days of raw records in `days`, written entries for 30 days in `entries`). Its wording is `W.JOURNAL` in `Words.lua`, a list of ways to say each beat; `Journal.LatestLine` and `Journal.LatelyGlance` are the only places the profile uses it.
- `AutoTRP/Sources/Deeds.lua` is Auto TRP's own record (quests turned in, masters felled), merged with Chronicle's in `Compose.Finished` and `Compose.Bosses`. Earned-name data (`W.EARNED_STORY`, `W.EARNED_QUEST`, `W.EARNED_BOSS`, `W.EARNED_BOUNTY`) is in `Words.lua`; quest titles and boss names are matched in English.
- `python3 tools/gen_rpgd_names.py /path/to/rpg-dialogue` regenerates `AutoTRP/Data/RPGDNames.lua` (town and story names; RPG Dialogue keeps those inside its private namespace, and its saved data only holds ids).
- All wording lives in `AutoTRP/Data/Words.lua`. Most of it is lists: one line is picked per character (and per hour, event or place), so adding a line adds variety. `W.SAY` holds the ways the glances and About phrase the facts they report. After `{they}`, write verb endings as `{s}`, `{es}` or `{ies}` (`{they} walk{s}`, `{they} carr{ies}`) so lines read right with he, she or they.
- `tests/unbacked_nouns.txt` lists things (hats, candles, beards, scars...) the look and mood word lists may not name outright. Gear goes through tags (`{head}`, `{mainhand}`, `{shield}`, `{pet}`...) that name the real item and drop the line when it isn't there (`U.Backed`).
- `tests/banned_phrases.txt` lists phrases Auto TRP must never write (stock AI phrasing and old tics), plus a few allowed only a handful of times. The offline test fails if the wording or a written profile uses one.
- About text uses Total RP 3's markup for colour (`{col:rrggbb}...{/col}`): quotes in a pale cream, items in their quality colour. The "Colour quotes and item names" setting turns it off.
- Writing follows Total RP 3's own pattern (`Core/Player.lua`): set the field, bump the section's version, fire `REGISTER_DATA_UPDATED` so other players fetch the new profile.
